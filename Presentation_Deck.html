Executive Summary

Project Vision & Strategic Key Points

CityWise AI empowers citizens across India—especially in rural and semi-urban regions—by providing a single, transparent digital portal to access localized department helplines, official state data, and eligible government welfare schemes without friction.

Strategic Key Objectives

  • 100% Pan-India Accessibility: Eliminate geographical barriers by unifying data for all 36 Indian States & UTs.
  • Hyper-Local Transparency: Provide village-level governance stats and local ward helpline numbers.
  • Click-Only Verified Search: Ensure clean zero-noise search results rendered exclusively upon user query.
  • Bilingual Voice Navigation: Integrated browser-native Speech Synthesis (Web Speech API) for non-literate support.

Target Audience & Reach

  • Farmers & Workers: PM-KISAN, MP Kisan Kalyan, Solar Pump Schemes.
  • Students & Youth: Bihar Student Credit Card, MP Sikho Kamao, UP Yuva Udyami.
  • Women & Girls: MP Ladli Behna, UP Kanya Sumangala, Bihar Kanya Utthan.
  • Vendors & Artisans: PM SVANidhi, PM Vishwakarma, PM Mudra Loans.
  • Senior Citizens: Indira Gandhi Pension, PM-JAY Ayushman Bharat.

Problem Statement

Current Challenges in Public Governance Delivery

Extreme Information Fragmentation

  • Over 500+ separate government websites exist across central and state ministries, making central discovery impossible for rural citizens.
  • Schemes are frequently renamed or modified without centralized documentation updates.

Lack of Hyper-Local Contact Data

  • Emergency helplines for essential local departments (Water Board, Power Discom 1912, PWD Roads, Sanitation) are difficult to locate at district/village level.
  • Citizens face bureaucratic red-tape when filing urgent local service complaints.

High Barrier & Language Friction

  • Complex government portals with heavy legal jargon confuse non-technical citizens seeking basic welfare assistance.
  • Lack of native voice-assist navigation hampers accessibility for non-literate rural populations.

Unfiltered Search Noise & Middlemen

  • Traditional search portals display dump lists without explicitly highlighting *"किसके लिए है"* (Target Eligibility Criteria).
  • Information asymmetry forces poor citizens to pay commission fees to unauthorized local agents/middlemen.

AI & Machine Learning Architecture

Machine Learning (ML / AI) Integration in CityWise AI

How AI & ML algorithms drive predictive scheme matching, NLP data ingestion, and speech processing:

1. Demographic Rule-Engine & Pattern Matcher

  • Calculates a multi-attribute Eligibility Compatibility Index based on citizen age, income, caste, and occupation.
  • Ranks schemes with highest approval probability at top of search results.

2. NLP Schema Ingestion & Classification

  • Processes unorganized JSON payloads from data.gov.in & myScheme.gov.in.
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) categorizes policies automatically into Agriculture, Female Welfare, Health, etc.

3. Speech Processing (TTS & NLP Audio)

  • Phonetic Speech Synthesis Engine converts native Hindi & English text to clear audio narration.
  • Enables zero-barrier accessibility for non-literate rural citizens.
